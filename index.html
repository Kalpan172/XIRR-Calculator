Primary Trade

Inputs

Amount as % of face or absolute ₹. Any unpaid balance is repaid at maturity.
Day-count base: a financial year (Apr–Mar) uses 366 only if it ends in a leap year and the schedule has a payment date in Feb or Mar of that year; otherwise 365. The base is set by each row's payment date.
Yield to Investor nets only Fees to Investor from the day-0 outflow (−Face + Fees to Investor). Total Cost to Issuer also deducts Other Fees (−Face + Fees to Investor + Other Fees). Instalments and manual tranches use month numbers counted from settlement; moratorium blocks principal only, not coupon.

Secondary Trade

Premium / discount on sale

Face value, coupon, fees, tenor and cashflows are taken from Primary Trade; change them there. Premium is the extra the investor pays above face (net of fees) to earn the "If Sold at" yield; a discount is shown as a negative.
