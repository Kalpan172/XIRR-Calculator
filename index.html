Amount as % of face or absolute ₹. Any unpaid balance is repaid at maturity.
Day-count base: a financial year (Apr–Mar) uses 366 only if it ends in a leap year and the schedule has a payment date in Feb or Mar of that year; otherwise 365. The base is set by each row's payment date.
Fees are netted from the day-0 outflow (as in the sheet: −Face + Fee). Instalments and manual tranches use month numbers counted from settlement; moratorium blocks principal only, not coupon.
